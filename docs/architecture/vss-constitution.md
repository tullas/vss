# VSS Constitution

## Purpose

These principles are durable tests for architecture and mission decisions.
They do not replace accepted ADRs, human judgment, or evidence. When a proposal
conflicts with a principle, it must identify the conflict and obtain an explicit
architecture decision rather than weakening the principle by convenience.

## Principles

1. **Human intent is sovereign.** VSS serves governed creative intent rather
   than silently replacing it.
2. **Authority is explicit.** Identity, capability, confidence, availability,
   and registration do not grant authority.
3. **Observation is not truth.** Observations remain qualified by evidence,
   provenance, uncertainty, and limitations.
4. **Interpretation is not fact.** Derived meaning stays distinguishable from
   source claims and independently verified facts.
5. **Confidence is not authority.** A score or threshold never grants truth,
   approval, authorization, or execution.
6. **An option is not a recommendation.** Alternatives remain inert until a
   separately governed evaluation gives them another meaning.
7. **A recommendation is not approval.** Approval and production authority are
   explicit decisions with accountable owners.
8. **Knowledge preserves provenance.** Accumulated knowledge retains enough
   exact lineage to interpret, qualify, revoke, and reassess it.
9. **Rights and source eligibility are first-class.** Accessibility never
   substitutes for an admitted right, purpose, classification, or retention
   basis.
10. **Models are replaceable; governed knowledge is strategic.** Model,
    provider, and implementation changes must not erase accumulated evidence or
    make authoritative meaning depend on one technology.
11. **Prefer simplicity until evidence requires complexity.** Use the smallest
    sufficient reliable architecture, considering lifetime rather than local
    cost.
12. **Logical separation does not imply service separation.** Boundaries define
    responsibility and authority before they define topology.
13. **Heavy data avoids semantic and control paths.** Authority and references
    remain bounded while large bytes follow governed workload-appropriate paths.
14. **Safety and quality outrank minimalism.** Security, reliability,
    correctness, recovery, durability, and required quality are not optional
    overhead.
15. **Experimental edges remain replaceable and non-authoritative.** Promotion
    requires explicit evidence and review.
16. **Frameworks are earned.** Repeated stable evidence across independent
    domains precedes shared abstraction.
17. **Each generation leaves clearer architecture and better knowledge.** New
    capability should improve evidence, legibility, and reversibility for the
    next generation.
18. **Humans retain final creative and production authority.** Any future
    delegation requires an explicit governed decision; autonomy is never
    inferred from technical capability.

## What VSS refuses to become

VSS refuses to become:

- a collection of disconnected AI tools;
- an infrastructure zoo accumulated without measured need;
- a Universal God Registry, Context, Work, Admission, or semantic object;
- an architecture dependent on one model, vendor, or experimental technology;
- an imitation or copying engine that loses reference provenance;
- a system where convenience silently weakens authority, eligibility,
  provenance, security, or quality; or
- a governance system whose paperwork grows faster than its decision value.

Movie production remains the proving application. Future scope is admitted by
evidence; possibility alone does not expand the mission.
