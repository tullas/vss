# M6.5: Bounded Admitted Cinematic Knowledge

M6.5 adds the smallest governed boundary from an accepted Shot/Cinematography
Lesson Candidate to an admitted Knowledge artifact. Admission is an explicit,
human-attributable decision; a candidate is never promoted automatically by
frequency, a provider, or a test.

The artifact is scoped to one project and the Shot/Cinematography domain. It
retains the exact Lesson Candidate, Pattern, Pattern Set, and Context lineage
needed to verify its origin. Only local manual or synthetic observation
provenance is eligible in this milestone.

Admitted Knowledge is not truth, a recommendation, creative approval, or
Runtime authority. It adds no causal, evaluative, prescriptive, or
cross-project meaning. The structured proposition is inherited from the
accepted recurrence or variation candidate.

Lifecycle events are immutable and human-attributable. Challenge, withdrawal,
revocation, and supersession make an artifact ineligible for current use while
leaving the historical artifact interpretable. Current-use validation fails
closed for unknown, malformed, missing, or substituted lifecycle and lineage
evidence. No persistence service, Knowledge registry, external learning, model
provenance, or automatic Knowledge promotion is introduced.
